# The IMPORTANCE OF DIGITAL SKILLS 

## INTRODUCTION 
Technology has become an important part of our everyday life. from communication and education to business and entertainment, digital tools influence how people work and interact.

## **What are digital skills?**
Digital skills are the abilities required to use digital devices, software, and online platforms effectively.

### Examples of digital skills
- *Computer Literacy*
- *Web development*
- *Graphic design*
- *Data analysis*
- *Digital marketing*

## Benefits of digital skills 
- Better career opportunities.
- Improved problem-solving abilities. 
- opportunities for entrepreneurship.

## Important digital skills to consider
- **Web development**
- **Graphic design**
- **Data analysis**

| Skill | Application | Example |

# How to start learning 
1. choose a skill. 
2. find learning resources. 
3. practise regularly.
4. work on projects.
5. learn from your mistakes.
6. build a portfolio of your work.

**Visit** [freecodecamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/) to explore programming lessons and practical exercises.

## Common challenges student may face 
- *lack of consistent practice*
- *limited access to computers or internet*
- *fear of making mistakes*
- *difficulty understanding technical concepts*
- *distractions from social media and entertainment*
---
However, these challenges can be managed through discipline, patience, and consistent effort.
